Who We Are

More Than Just a Phone Shop

Global Phonelinz Systems Limited (GPSL) is a multi-branch technology and telecommunications business headquartered in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, Nigeria. We have been serving individuals, families and businesses with premium phones, accessories, telecom services, and digital solutions since our founding.

Our team of trained professionals operates across five branches, ensuring you always have access to genuine products, expert service, and competitive prices — right in your community.
